# FMCI Network beta reveal

A guided session of approximately 25–30 minutes, including hands-on time. Open the HTML file in a browser. Use arrow keys, Full screen, and N for notes. Print / PDF exports the slide deck without notes.

Before the session: prepare your invite link, sign in to a demo account, choose appropriate posts and a consenting message recipient, test an event and video, and close private conversations. Invitations are shared separately and are not embedded in this deck.

## 1. FMCI Network

Welcome everyone and thank them for being early users. Introduce the app as a way to maintain relationships and share ministry life between gatherings. Explain that this is a beta and that honest feedback will shape what happens next. Allow 1 minute.

## 2. Our network, closer together

Ask: How do you currently find a member or hear what is happening across the network? Take two brief answers. Connect their examples to the app without claiming it replaces their church or existing relationships. Allow 2 minutes.

## 3. A first look, together

Set expectations for a 20–30 minute session. Explain that you will alternate between the deck and the actual app. Keep questions welcome, but reserve detailed troubleshooting for the end. Allow 1 minute.

## 4. Your invitation

Displayed invite link: https://tinyurl.com/fmcinet

Share the invitation through your chosen channel now. Do not show an invitation token on a projected slide. Let participants follow the actual prompts. Help anyone who already has an account sign in instead. Do not promise a public App Store or TestFlight link. Allow 3 minutes.

## 5. Your profile

Switch to the app and open your own profile. Show where to edit it. Explain that some ministry roles may need approval and do not imply that every signup is already verified. Give users a moment to review their own profile. Allow 2 minutes.

## 6. Member Directory

Open Member Directory. Point out the total count beside the title and explain that the filtered result count appears separately. Search for a consenting beta participant or use your own profile. Avoid projecting private contact details. Allow 2 minutes.

## 7. The network feed

Show the real feed. Explain All Network and the ministry feed when a primary ministry is configured. Demonstrate one reaction and one brief comment only on content appropriate for the audience. Show the recommendation carousels without claiming personalization beyond their actual behavior. Allow 2 minutes.

## 8. Prayer and testimonies

Open Prayer and Testimonies. Show the available posting controls and explain the audience displayed by the app. Encourage users to obtain permission before sharing someone else’s story or sensitive details. Avoid promising that all content is private. Allow 2 minutes.

## 9. Messages

Demonstrate messaging with a participant who has agreed to receive a message. Let users send a short hello to another tester. Do not project existing private conversations. If push permissions arise, explain that users choose whether to allow notifications. Allow 2 minutes.

## 10. Ministries and groups

Show one appropriate ministry page and an available group. Explain only the membership actions actually offered to the current account. Do not imply that every group is open or that all users can publish as a ministry. Allow 1–2 minutes.

## 11. Events and resources

Open an actual event, then the resource library. Show the thumbnail and tap-to-play behavior for YouTube. Explain that external resources may open in a browser. Keep the demo to one event and one resource. Allow 2 minutes.

## 12. A few minutes to explore

Give the room 3 minutes. Invite users to choose tasks that fit their comfort level rather than forcing a public post. Watch for confusion and record it. Avoid explaining every control before users have a chance to try it.

## 13. Respect for one another

Show the actual report/block entry points and Safety & Support. Explain that moderators review reports without promising a response time. State that invite-only access does not make every post or uploaded item confidential. Allow 1 minute.

## 14. Your experience will shape the app

Invite specific examples. Ask users to distinguish bugs from suggestions. Use the existing support email for now unless you have announced another beta feedback channel. Never request passwords, invitation tokens, or screenshots of private conversations. Ask: What would make you return this week? Allow 2 minutes.

## 15. Welcome to FMCI Network

Close with thanks and open the floor. Repeat where you shared the invite link and how to reach support. Do not announce a launch date, beta deadline, or promised feature unless you have confirmed it separately. Allow remaining time for questions.

## Content basis

Feature descriptions follow the current repository components and documentation. No unreleased pagination, crash-reporting feature, store availability, release date, or beta deadline is promised. Support address comes from SafetySupportView.
